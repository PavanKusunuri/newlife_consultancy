FAQ

Questions We Often Hear

What kinds of roles do you recruit for?

We handle both IT and non-IT hiring, from entry-level and mid-level positions to senior leadership, on permanent and contract bases.

How quickly can I expect to see candidates?

After the discovery call, we typically share a first shortlist of screened profiles within a few working days, depending on the role and seniority.

Can you manage payroll for a small team?

Yes. Our payroll service works for teams of any size and covers salary processing, payslips and statutory deductions and filings.

What does an HR audit involve?

We review your policies, documentation, compliance and people metrics, then deliver a clear report with prioritised recommendations.

How do we get started?

Send us a message using the form below or email us. We will schedule a short discovery call to understand your requirements.
